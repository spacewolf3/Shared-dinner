Move the clock forward to watch statuses end and open hours switch on and off.

What we're testing

  • Statuses expire. Every status has an end time. Is 2 hours the right default?
  • Notes expire too. "Made ice cream!" never lingers into Tuesday.
  • Groups. Anyone can make a group and invite people to it. The sample groups show neighbors who can pop over in minutes and friends who live farther away. Does the same app work for both?
  • High trust identity. Anyone can pick any house in "You are." A phone-number login comes later.
  • Text first. Is a middle option useful, or does it bring back the awkward asking?
  • Notify me. Tap the bell on a house. In the real app these would be push notifications or texts.
