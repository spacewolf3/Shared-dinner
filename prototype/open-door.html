Open Door

Prototype controls

Move the clock forward to watch statuses expire and open hours switch on and off.

What we're testing

  • Statuses expire. Every "Come on by" has an end time, then the house falls back to its open hours. Is 2 hours the right default?
  • Notes expire too. The note goes away with the status, so "made ice cream!" never lingers into Tuesday.
  • High trust identity. Anyone can pick any house in "You are." Fine for a beta with friends; a phone-number login like Partiful's comes later.
  • Text first vs. come on by. Is a middle option useful, or does it bring back the awkward asking?
  • Notify me. Tap the bell on a house. In the real app these would be push notifications or texts.
